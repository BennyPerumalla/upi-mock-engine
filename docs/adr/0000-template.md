## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the requirement, or the defect — not a
restatement of the problem domain. Cite the design document section or the NPCI
behaviour if there is one. A reader who disagrees with the decision should be able to
tell from this section alone whether they disagree with the reasoning or with the
premises.

## Decision

One paragraph, present tense, active voice: *we do X*. Name the modules and the
identifiers, so the ADR can be checked against the tree rather than believed.

## Consequences

What this buys, and what it costs. The cost section is the one that matters — an ADR
with no downsides recorded is an advertisement. Include what becomes harder, what a
future contributor must not do, and what would have to change to reverse the
decision.

## Alternatives considered

Each with the reason it lost. "Simpler" is not a reason; the reason is what the
simpler thing fails to guarantee. An alternative that was never seriously in play
does not belong here.
